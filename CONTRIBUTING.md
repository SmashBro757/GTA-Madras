# Contributing

These notes record a paused personal idea. Venkat is writing them down. There is no team, no company, and no group of maintainers behind the name. The idea is not in development. For roughly the next month he is occupied with leaving a job and with career matters. He has set no date to resume, and these notes are not a commitment that he will.

Interest from other people is not being sought. Nothing on this page creates a role. If the idea ever continues, he intends it as personal, unpaid, and open to community contribution. That intention is not a present invitation to build, host, research costs, design multiplayer, analyze someone else's video, or publicize the idea.

The repository is not licensed yet, and it does not contain a game.

## What is useful now

Until a license is chosen, the useful contributions are corrections and cautions on the notes:

- a passage misstates the intent, or invents a decision the notes do not make
- a description of Chennai flattens it, caricatures it, or invents history
- the working title needs a clearer warning, or a descriptive sketch is confusing
- the line between this idea, the sister repository [Project Madras](https://github.com/SmashBro757/Project-Madras), and the existing Project Madras game has been blurred

GitHub issues, and pull requests that only change documentation, are fine ways to offer that. There is no review rota and no promise of a reply. Silence means no one is staffing the repository. It is not a verdict on the suggestion.

## What to hold back

Please do not open pull requests that add code, art, audio, maps, data, or other assets. Code, assets, and data may eventually need licenses of their own, separate from these notes and from each other. A pull request does not create a license. A public repository does not give you, or anyone else, the right to reuse what is already here or what you might add.

Please do not add a `LICENSE` file on your own. License selection is pending on purpose.

A small public joinable proof of concept is a future aspiration for after career commitments. Sooner rather than later applies only once those commitments allow, and it is not a date. The proof is not authorized work. Please do not start it, design multiplayer for it, or estimate hosting costs. Engine, hosting, and release choices have not been made. These notes are not a task list.

Please do not treat the notes as a request for implementation, outreach, or a review of the existing Project Madras video.

## Tone of suggestions

Humor about an early build's roughness is an optional suggestion in [docs/vision.md](docs/vision.md). It is not a request for jokes, and it is not a request for jokes at the city's expense.

Keep political figures, parties, and public convoys out of suggestions. A convoy idea tied to Vijay was already considered, flagged for the risk of offense, and left unapproved.

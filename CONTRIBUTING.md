# Contributing

These are one person's initial notes. Venkat is writing them down. There is no team, no company, and no group of maintainers behind the name. Interest from other people is welcome. Nothing on this page creates a role for you or for anyone else.

If the project continues, it is intended to be open to community contribution. It is not licensed yet, and it does not contain a game. The invitation is real. The repository is not yet a place where that invitation can be acted out in code.

## What is useful now

Until a license is chosen, the useful contributions are corrections and cautions on the notes:

- a passage misstates the intent, or invents a decision the notes do not make
- a description of Chennai flattens it, caricatures it, or invents history
- the working title needs a clearer warning, or a descriptive sketch is confusing
- the line between this project and [Project Madras](https://github.com/SmashBro757/Project-Madras) has been blurred

GitHub issues, and pull requests that only change documentation, are fine ways to offer that. There is no review rota and no promise of a reply. Silence means no one is staffing the repository. It is not a verdict on the suggestion.

## What to hold back

Please do not open pull requests that add code, art, audio, maps, data, or other assets. Code, assets, and data may eventually need licenses of their own, separate from these notes and from each other. A pull request does not create a license. A public repository does not give you, or anyone else, the right to reuse what is already here or what you might add.

Please do not add a `LICENSE` file on your own. License selection is pending on purpose.

The tiny explorable proof of concept is the intended first concrete step, and its scope is undecided. Please do not treat it as specified work, and please do not start it in this repository. Engine, hosting, and release choices have not been made. These notes are not a task list.

## Tone of suggestions

Humor about an early build's roughness is an optional suggestion in [docs/vision.md](docs/vision.md). It is not a request for jokes, and it is not a request for jokes at the city's expense.

Keep political figures, parties, and public convoys out of suggestions. A convoy idea tied to Vijay was already considered, flagged for the risk of offense, and left unapproved.

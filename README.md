# GTA Madras

*Working title only. Provisional, and open to replacement.*

Notes toward a compressed, geographically selective Chennai that a person can explore. The aim is the city's essence, character, and air, with a realistic visual ambition.

**This repository is documentation.** It contains no game code, no engine decision, no assets, no deployment setup, and no roadmap.

The words "GTA Madras" borrow another series' initials as genre shorthand. That borrowing is uneasy, and the title is not settled. Descriptive sketches live in [docs/naming.md](docs/naming.md). No name there has been checked for availability, and listing one is not a claim that it can be used.

This project is independent of Rockstar Games. The working title is not a claim of connection. The project does not use that company's stories, characters, or assets. Marks echoed by the working title stay with their owners.

## What the notes are aiming at

Chennai is personal to Venkat. The aim is to do that city justice: recognizable, lived-in, and visually believable, with room for the IT corridor and older industry, the beaches, temples and devotional life, cultural history, historic buildings that are still standing, and family life at different ages. Which of those a first version can hold is undecided.

The scale is deliberately manageable. A one-to-one city is outside this project's aim. The sister project, [Project Madras](https://github.com/SmashBro757/Project-Madras), is for a geographically grounded present-day Chennai and a historical reconstruction, in which time, terrain, and infrastructure are meant to change accurately. Both are *intended* as open-source community projects. They do not share a brief. [docs/vision.md](docs/vision.md) draws the line. [docs/references.md](docs/references.md) records the other city efforts these notes are thinking with.

An optional, nonpolitical bit of humor at the start of a rough early version has been suggested and not adopted. Political set pieces are outside the project. The vision notes say so plainly.

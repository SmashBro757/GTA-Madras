# GTA Madras

*Working title only. Unresolved. A paused personal idea, not an active project.*

**Status, clarified 7 November 2026, 20:29 IST.** This repository is a personal pet project on paper. It is an idea. It is not active development. Venkat is occupied for roughly the next month with leaving a job and with career matters. No date has been set to pick the idea up again, and these notes are not a commitment to restart.

The idea is a fictionalized city inspired by Chennai. The geography would be much smaller than the real city. Venkat's 80/20 is a shorthand for that cut: concentrate on the core highlights that carry the lived feel, and spend the space on a density of interesting places, activities, and micro-interactions. The visual ambition is realistic. Which highlights a later version could actually hold is undecided.

"GTA-style" here means that genre and that fictionalization. It does not mean borrowed assets or borrowed branding.

**This repository is documentation of that paused idea.** It contains no game code, no engine decision, no assets, no deployment, and no roadmap.

## Three different things

Chennai is personal to Venkat. The hope, if the idea is ever made, is to do a lived city justice inside a fiction: its character and its air, without caricature, and without claiming that Chennai outranks any other city. The highlights he wants the fiction to be able to hold are the IT corridor and older industry, the beaches, temples and devotional life, cultural history, historic buildings that are still standing, and family life at different ages. That is variety inside a small fiction. It is not a map of the real city.

The same names point at three separate efforts.

**This repository** is Venkat's paused idea of a compact, fictionalized, Chennai-inspired city. He intends it, if it ever continues, as personal, unpaid, and a community effort. Open source remains an intention, and no license has been selected. It is not in development.

**The sister repository, [Project Madras](https://github.com/SmashBro757/Project-Madras),** is also his, and the same clarification treats it as an idea and a personal pet project rather than active development. Its brief is different: a geographically grounded present-day Chennai, and a historical reconstruction in which time, terrain, and infrastructure change accurately. [docs/vision.md](docs/vision.md) draws that line. These notes do not speak for the other repository.

**The existing game** in [Project Madras - OpenWorld Gameplay Showcase](https://youtu.be/ra-lYQAhbB8?si=ClY5YFNWclMkVFTL), on the Project Madras channel, is other people's work. Venkat has watched it. He describes years of hard work, and he loves their project. He also describes it as an existing independent commercial Chennai game. Makers of a commercial game may be motivated by love; these notes take that as given. His idea is a different object, a small fiction inspired by the city. It is not a competing full-city recreation, and it is not an imitation of their game. The video is not a source in these notes for anyone's scope, cost, or history. [docs/references.md](docs/references.md) records the link and that limit.

A small proof of concept that other people could join, publicly, is a future aspiration. It belongs after career commitments, and the wish is that it would come sooner rather than later once those commitments allow. It is not authorization to build one now. What it would contain is undecided. Server and hosting expense is a future question of feasibility. These notes contain no estimates and no research on that question.

An optional, nonpolitical bit of humor at the start of a rough early version has been suggested and not adopted. Political set pieces are outside the idea. The vision notes say so plainly.

## Name

The words "GTA Madras" are an unresolved working title. Venkat is uneasy about piggybacking on Grand Theft Auto branding. Descriptive sketches live in [docs/naming.md](docs/naming.md). No name there has been checked for availability, and listing one is not a claim that it can be used.

This idea is independent of Rockstar Games. The working title is not a claim of connection. It does not use that company's stories, characters, or assets, and it does not borrow branding. Marks echoed by the working title stay with their owners.

# References

Two public efforts are noted here because they make a tradeoff concrete: production scale, a recognizable city, and realism. They pull against each other. Any compressed Chennai faces that same tradeoff. These notes do not pick a point on it, and they do not treat either effort as a template, a partner, or an affiliation.

They are also not a claim that this project is further along, or that Chennai is a better subject than Hyderabad or Bengaluru. What someone played, and what a page or a post merely says, are kept separate below.

## Hyd World

Public page: <https://gtahyd.in/>

A read of the public page on 7 November 2026 returned the title **Hyd World**, the place line **HITEC City · Hyderabad**, and the labels Download, Start, City, and Streets. These notes take that, and no more, from the page itself.

Venkat's firsthand account of playing it:

- It was freely and publicly playable, and it was multiplayer.
- Another player shot him.
- An area of Hyderabad where he had stayed was recognizable, and still rough.
- A bike passed with a player-chosen name, Revanth Reddy. The name was not scripted. It made him laugh.

That account is the context for the optional early opening in the vision notes. The laugh belongs to someone else's unscripted multiplayer session. It is not a brief to put political names into this project. The vision notes leave political set pieces out, including a convoy idea that was raised and then dropped.

## A GTA-style Bengaluru, day 20

Post, by Prajwal Shetty ([@shettyprajwal_](https://x.com/shettyprajwal_)), 6 November 2026:

<https://x.com/shettyprajwal_/status/2118664731456151646>

The post's text includes these lines:

> Day 20 of building GTA style Bengaluru game […]
>
> Flashback mission - 2018 - Majestic!

The same post describes ongoing work on weather, missions, cinematics, crowds, and sound, and names a map area taking in Ulsoor lake, Majestic, Racecourse, Anand Rao circle, and surroundings. It identifies Unreal Engine as that project's tool. This repository has chosen no engine, and the mention is not a recommendation.

The post carries a video. The video was not watched for these notes. The historical and visual accuracy of the 2018 Majestic flashback, and of the named areas, is unverified here. The post is its author's description of his own work.

## How to read the pair

In Venkat's account, Hyd World could already be entered, and a place he knew was already recognizable, while the result was still rough. Prajwal Shetty's post describes a heavier production aimed at a recognizable Bengaluru, including a dated flashback, at a stated early day count, with the picture itself unchecked. Together they are a reminder of the tradeoff. They are not a specification.
